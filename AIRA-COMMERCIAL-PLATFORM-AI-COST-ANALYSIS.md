## 4. Financial Recommendations
1. **Rollover Policy**: Unused monthly credits roll over up to a maximum cap of 2× the monthly allotment (Plus: max 6,000; Pro: max 15,000) to maintain financial liability boundaries.
2. **Top-Up Credits**: Purchased top-up credits never expire and are consumed after monthly grants.
3. **Fair-Use Ceiling**: The daily standard chat limit (15, 50, 150) effectively protects the platform from runaway scraping or infinite retry loops without harming normal human conversational workflows.
